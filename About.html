Our Story

Founded in 2024 by Apelele, Apelele Hair Collection was born out of a passion for high-quality, long lasting hair extensions. Tired of low quality hair and human hair that has been processed a tad bit much being available, Apelele got ethical suppliers in Vietnam.

Today, we proudly supply women around Gqeberha and some part of the Eastern Cape with great quality virgin weaves that can be dyed, bleached, and styled for years with proper care.

Our Mission

To empower women in Gqeberha by providing them with quality, authentic raw Vietnamese weaves and personalized wig-customization services.

Our Standards

  • Unprocessed Quality:Cuticles stay fully aligned to minimize shedding and tangling.
  • Longevity:Our Vietnamesehair can last up to more than 3 years when cared for properly.
  • Personalized Care:Every wig and weave becomes customized according to client cap size and what the client prefers.
